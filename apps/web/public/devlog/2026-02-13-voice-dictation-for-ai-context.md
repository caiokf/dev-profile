Voice dictation has become awesome lately, and I use it daily to collaborate with AI coding agents like Claude.

When reviewing AI-generated code changes, I keep dictation running and think out loud. As I test, explore, and evaluate the code, I capture every thought, question, and observation. This could run for even 5 minutes, depending on the complexity of what I'm reviewing.

Instead of trying to reconstruct my thinking or provide vague feedback, I have a rich transcript of my actual review process. The AI gets context about what I tested, what felt off, and why certain approaches might not work.

This thinking-out-loud approach bridges the gap between human intuition and AI execution. The context matters enormously for getting better results on subsequent iterations.
