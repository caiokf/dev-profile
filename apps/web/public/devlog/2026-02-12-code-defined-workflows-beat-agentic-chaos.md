Fully agentic workflows feel impressive until you hit production. The randomness compounds at every step, turning predictable tasks into debugging nightmares. At least in software engineering.

## Deterministic Wins

Well-defined, code-driven workflows give you something agentic systems can't: reliability. When LLMs output structured JSON, you can validate each step before moving forward. No surprises, no "it worked yesterday" moments.

Permissions become surgical too. Each workflow step gets exactly the access it needs, nothing more. Compare that to giving an agent broad permissions and hoping it behaves.

Tracing becomes easier when we execute LLM steps from code. Stack traces beat "the agent decided to do something different" every time.
